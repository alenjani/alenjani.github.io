About

Mechanical engineer who took a left turn into ML and never went back. PhD at Purdue on decision-making under uncertainty in physical systems, then postdocs at Stanford on multimodal ML for global health, and NASA STRI / Purdue (RETHi) on a space-habitat digital twin. Different domains, same job: build something that has to make a fast decision on real data without any of the inputs being clean.

The work splits into three lanes that share more DNA than they look. Multimodal detection & change-detection across vision and other evidence streams — fusion pipelines, custom data collection, decisions calibrated to deployment. Digital twins & risk-informed decision-making for complex coupled systems — modeling, trade-space analysis, portfolio-style ranking under uncertainty. And personalization & ranking systems in production — recsys at scale, LLM-based evaluation, counterfactual simulation. Based in the SF Bay Area.
