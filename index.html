What I work on

Production recommender systems

End-to-end two-tower retrieval pipelines: model design, contrastive losses, hard negative mining, ONNX serving, HNSW vector indexes, A/B test design, online–offline metric alignment.

LLM evaluation & quality gating

Multi-dimensional human-proxy judging pipelines using Chain-of-Thought scoring across orthogonal latent dimensions to automate quality gates for recsys releases.

Counterfactual offline simulation

High-fidelity sim platforms with LLM agents, time-travel historical sampling, and self-adaptive metrics — built to minimize sim-to-real gap before deployment decisions.

Digital twins & risk modeling

Physics-informed simulation, Monte Carlo trade studies, computer vision for high-throughput inspection. NASA Space Habitat platform; published research on built-environment risk.

Things I've built

Real-time recommendation platform

  • Two-tower retrieval at production scale, trained on billions of interactions.
  • Sub-10ms retrieval over hundreds of thousands of items in HNSW vector search; ONNX serving for several-times-faster inference vs. native PyTorch.
  • Ranking-aware contrastive loss (InfoNCE + same-taxonomy hard-negative mining) for fine-grained attribute discrimination.
  • MosaicML Streaming for sharded reads across 8× A100s; warm-start training to cut compute cost down to a fraction of cold-start runs.

LLM-as-a-judge for recommendations

  • Multi-dimensional evaluation pipeline that quantifies semantic complementarity in recsys outputs.
  • Declarative "human-proxy" judging across orthogonal latent dimensions with Chain-of-Thought reasoning — automated quality gating before release.

Counterfactual offline simulation

  • High-fidelity simulator integrated with LLM agents for autonomous user behavior.
  • Time-travel historical sampling and self-adaptive metric tuning to keep the sim-to-real gap honest before production deployment decisions.

Computer vision for industrial inspection

  • End-to-end CV inspection across CTQ checkpoints — automated defect detection and station health monitoring.
  • Multi-class object detection in PyTorch with operational decision logic tuned to minimize false positives in high-throughput environments.
  • Leakage-safe data workflow on GCP/GCS + Label Studio; targeted hard-negative mining to handle lighting, misalignment, and class imbalance from real plant floors.

Space habitat digital twin (NASA STRI)

  • The CDCM platform — thermodynamic and structural models coupled to simulate deep-space habitat resilience.
  • Active thermal control, power, and structural integrity under stochastic disturbances (micrometeoroid impacts, thermal cycling).
  • Large-scale Monte Carlo trade studies against NPV and risk metrics for mission planning.

About

Mechanical engineer turned ML person. PhD at Purdue on AI-based decision-making in socio-technical systems (computer vision, deep learning, stochastic simulation), then postdocs at Stanford (multimodal ML for global health) and NASA STRI / Purdue (space habitat digital twin). The thread through all of it has been the same: building systems that have to make good decisions under uncertainty, fast, on real data.

These days I spend most of my time on production recommender systems and the evaluation problem around them — what to actually measure, how to use LLMs as judges without fooling yourself, and how to keep offline simulators honest about online behavior.
